I am a third-year undergraduate student at Wenzhou-Kean University, studying Mathematical Sciences with a Data Analytics option and expecting to graduate in 2028.

My research interests include continual learning, deep learning, and AI for healthcare. I am particularly interested in how medical AI models can adapt to new information and changing diagnostic definitions while retaining previously learned capabilities.

Since February 2026, I have been an Undergraduate Research Assistant in the AI4Healthcare Lab at Kean University, advised by Prof. Kuan Huang. Previously, I was a visiting student in Prof. Lin Yang’s research group at Westlake University, working with Yu Bai on enzyme activity prediction and computational pathology.
